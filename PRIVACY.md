# Privacy

OmniAPO processes audio and configuration data locally on the Windows computer where it is installed.

## Network behavior

OmniAPO itself has no update check, telemetry, analytics, advertising, crash-upload, account, or cloud-sync service. The distributed binaries do not link a networking library.

## Local data

Configuration files, presets, logs, and diagnostic output remain on the local computer unless the user deliberately shares them. Audio is processed in the Windows audio pipeline and is not sent to OmniVex.

## Support and external services

GitHub, Patreon, Ko-fi, and email are separate services with their own privacy terms. When requesting support, remove usernames, device names, local paths, account identifiers, and other personal information from screenshots and logs.

Questions about this policy can be sent to [omnivex@theomnigrid.biz](mailto:omnivex@theomnigrid.biz).
